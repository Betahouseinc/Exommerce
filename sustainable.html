What we hold ourselves to

Seven working rules

None of these are new ideas. The work is in keeping to them.

Take on only what we can deliver

We match commitments to what our team can actually carry. Saying "not yet" is part of doing the work well.

Improve before replacing

Most businesses already have technology that mostly works. We start there, and only suggest a rebuild when it clearly pays for itself.

Human-led, AI-assisted

AI speeds up the routine work. Decisions, judgement and relationships stay with people.

Keep costs lean

We use free and open-source tools where they do the job, and we flag anything that would add a recurring bill before it's added.

Build our own products

Alongside consulting we build products of our own, starting with LeadGenAI. A second line of work means we never need to push services a client doesn't need.

Hand over cleanly

What we set up is documented and handed over, so you can run it without us.

Say only what we can back

No borrowed logos or inflated numbers. The case studies on our site are public examples, cited as such.
