      FAQ

      Before you book

      How does an engagement start?

      With a discovery call. If there's a fit, we do a short assessment of your current setup and send a written plan covering scope, timeline and cost. Nothing starts until you've agreed to it.

      How do you price your work?

      It depends on the work: a fixed scope for assessments and one-off builds, and a monthly fee for ongoing managed support. Either way you get a written quote before anything starts.

      Can you take over an infrastructure someone else built?

      Yes. We start by reviewing what's running, document it, fix the riskiest gaps first, and then take on day-to-day running or hand it back to your team.

      Which clouds and tools do you work with?

      AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, plus Kubernetes, Terraform, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI and the monitoring stack you already use. We're vendor-agnostic and recommend what fits, not what we resell.

      Do you work in our time zone?

      We're based in Bengaluru and overlap with US mornings, full UK and EU afternoons, and most of the working day in the Middle East and APAC. On-call cover is agreed per engagement.

      Who owns the code and infrastructure?

      You do. We sign an NDA on request, assign IP to you, and document everything, so nothing depends on us staying.
