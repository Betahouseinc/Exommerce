FAQ

Common questions

How does an engagement start?

With a friendly call about your challenge. If we're a good fit, we take a short look at your current setup and send a written plan covering scope, timeline and cost. Nothing begins until you're happy with it.

How do you keep our systems and data secure?

We work with least-privilege access you can revoke at any time, keep secrets out of code, and review and log every change. We're happy to sign an NDA and to follow your own security policies.

How do you price your work?

A fixed price for assessments and one-off projects, or a simple monthly fee for ongoing support. Either way, you'll get a clear written quote before anything starts.

Can you take over infrastructure someone else built?

Absolutely. We get to know what's running, document it and fix the most urgent gaps first. Then we can look after it day to day or hand it back to your team.
