Security & access

Exactly what we can and can't see

We use AWS-managed read-only policies: ViewOnlyAccess, AWSBillingReadOnlyAccess and ComputeOptimizerReadOnlyAccess. We deliberately avoid the broader ReadOnlyAccess policy, which can read S3 data.

✅ What we can see

  • Resource configuration (instance types, volumes, IPs, databases)
  • Cost & usage data from AWS Cost Explorer
  • AWS Compute Optimizer recommendations
  • Utilization metrics needed to spot idle resources

What we can't do

  • Read your data: S3 objects, database rows, secrets
  • Create, modify or delete anything
  • Access anything after you delete the role
  • Keep your data: deleted within 30 days of delivery

Our own audit account is protected with MFA and CloudTrail logging, and is used only for client audits. NDA or data-processing agreement available on request: hello@exommerce.online.

FAQ

Questions CTOs ask us

What exactly do you get access to?
A single IAM role with three AWS-managed read-only policies (ViewOnlyAccess, AWSBillingReadOnlyAccess, ComputeOptimizerReadOnlyAccess). The role can only be assumed by our audit account using an External ID unique to your order. We can't read S3 objects, database contents or secrets, and we can't change anything.
What's free, and what do I pay for?
The scan and the summary are free: your monthly savings figure, how it splits between cleanup, resizing and commitments, and the biggest items we found. The full report (every resource, the evidence behind each figure, dry-run scripts, the plan and a walkthrough call) is $299 at the founding price. You decide after you've seen the summary.
When do I get my summary?
Within 24 hours of your read-only access working, not from when you book. We email you as soon as we've confirmed access.
Can you check again next quarter or next year?
Yes. Choose "every quarter" or "every year" when you book and we'll remind you when it's due. Keep the findings file from your report, and the next one shows what you fixed and what's new. Returning clients pay less for the full report.
Who runs the cleanup scripts?
You do. Scripts run in dry-run mode by default (they only list what they would change) and take a snapshot before any deletion. You review, you decide, you execute. Want us to do it? Add the "we apply the fixes" option.
What if some resources are idle on purpose?
Tag them Environment=DR, CostScope=Exclude or exommerce:skip and the scan leaves them out: a disaster-recovery standby isn't waste. Tell us about any other tags you already use for this.
Do you support Azure and Google Cloud?
AWS is live today. Azure and Google Cloud are in final testing. Join the waitlist and we'll notify you first.
What if our account spends less than $3,000/month?
The free scan still works. Below about $3,000 a month there's often too little waste for the full report to be worth it, and we'll tell you so.
Can you sign an NDA or DPA?
Yes. Email hello@exommerce.online before or after booking.
How do we remove access afterwards?
Delete the IAM role; your summary email includes the one command. You can do it as soon as the summary arrives, because the scan is done by then.
