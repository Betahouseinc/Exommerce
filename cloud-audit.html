Security & access

Exactly what we can and can't see

We use AWS-managed read-only policies: ViewOnlyAccess, AWSBillingReadOnlyAccess and ComputeOptimizerReadOnlyAccess. We deliberately avoid the broader ReadOnlyAccess policy, which can read S3 data.

✅ What we can see

  • Resource configuration (instance types, volumes, IPs, databases)
  • Cost & usage data from AWS Cost Explorer
  • AWS Compute Optimizer recommendations
  • Utilization metrics needed to spot idle resources
  • Which services, regions and environments you use

What we can't do

  • Read your data: S3 objects, database rows, secrets
  • Create, modify or delete anything
  • Access anything after you delete the role
  • Hold access keys or passwords: access is a role you control
  • Keep your account details: deleted within 30 days of delivery

Our own audit account is protected with MFA and CloudTrail logging, and is used only for client audits. NDA or data-processing agreement available on request: hello@exommerce.online.

FAQ

Questions CTOs ask us

What exactly do you get access to?
A single IAM role with three AWS-managed read-only policies (ViewOnlyAccess, AWSBillingReadOnlyAccess, ComputeOptimizerReadOnlyAccess). The role can only be assumed by our audit account using an External ID unique to your audit. We never ask for access keys or passwords. We can't read S3 objects, database contents or secrets, and we can't change anything.
What's free, and what do I pay for?
The audit is free: your potential monthly savings, how they split between cleanup, rightsizing and commitments, the biggest opportunities and their severity, and a snapshot of your stack. If you want help acting on it, the ₹25,000 Cloud Optimization Engagement validates each opportunity against your environment, prioritizes them, and implements the ones you approve. It covers the opportunities from your audit; anything beyond that is scoped separately. Ongoing Cloud Optimization & Support is available after that. You decide after you've seen your findings.
When do I get my initial findings?
Within 24 hours of successful AWS access, not from when you request the audit. We work from the cost and usage history AWS already keeps, so there's nothing to install and no waiting period. We email you as soon as we've confirmed access.
How firm are the savings figures?
They're potential savings until validated. In the engagement, each one is checked against how the resource is actually used before it becomes a recommendation, and only the recommendations you approve are implemented. Savings Plans are sized on your usage after cleanup and rightsizing, never on today's bill alone.
What does Ongoing Cloud Optimization & Support include?
Recurring cost reviews, quarterly or yearly, each compared with your previous audit so you see what you fixed and what's new, plus ongoing optimization, infrastructure efficiency work and implementation support. We agree the scope and price with you before it starts.
Who makes the changes?
Nothing changes automatically: the audit is read-only. In the engagement, changes are made only for the opportunities you approve, in a window you choose, with a rollback step for each. Scripts run in dry-run mode by default (they only list what they would change) and take a snapshot before any deletion.
What if some resources are idle on purpose?
Tag them Environment=DR, CostScope=Exclude or exommerce:skip and the audit leaves them out: a disaster-recovery standby isn't waste. We also check for scheduled jobs, auto-scaling groups, DNS records pointing at a resource, and addresses partners may have allowlisted, before anything is recommended for removal. Tell us about any other tags you already use for this.
Do you support Azure and Google Cloud?
AWS is live today. Azure and Google Cloud are in final testing. Join the waitlist and we'll notify you first.
What if our account spends less than $3,000/month?
The free audit still works. Below about $3,000 a month there's often too little waste for a paid engagement to be worth it, and we'll tell you so.
Can you sign an NDA or DPA?
Yes. Email hello@exommerce.online before or after requesting your audit.
How do we remove access afterwards?
Delete the IAM role; the email with your initial findings includes the one command. You can do it as soon as your findings arrive, because the scan is done by then.
