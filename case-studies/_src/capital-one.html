01The challenge

Regulated businesses often assume that control means slowness: manual approvals, change boards and long provisioning queues. At Capital One, application releases happened monthly or quarterly, and standing up a development environment could take months [1].

02What Capital One did

  • Automation as the control. Security and compliance checks were built into the platform and pipeline, so they ran on every change instead of in a review queue.
  • Rebuilt for the cloud. Around 80% of its nearly 2,000 applications were rebuilt from the ground up to run natively in the cloud [1].
  • Self-service environments. Engineers could provision a development environment in minutes rather than waiting months.
  • A long-term commitment. The migration ran over eight years and ended with all eight on-premises data centres closed [1].

03The results

  • Releases went from monthly or quarterly to multiple times a day [1].
  • Critical incident resolution time and transaction errors were both cut by 50% [1].
  • Development environments that took three months now take minutes [1].
  • Disaster recovery improved by 70% in testing exercises [1].
The lesson: in a regulated business, automation doesn't weaken control, it proves it. A pipeline that records every check is a better audit trail than a spreadsheet of approvals.

04At your scale: a 90-day plan

You aren't rebuilding 2,000 applications over eight years. One product team can adopt the same pattern in a quarter.

Days 1–30 · Gap assessmentDays 31–60 · Guardrails as codeDays 61–90 · Evidence by default
  • Map the controls you're assessed on (SOC 2, ISO 27001 or a customer questionnaire)
  • Inventory access, secrets and environments
  • Rank gaps by audit risk
  • A landing zone built as code, with separate accounts per environment
  • Least-privilege access and single sign-on
  • Secrets moved into a managed vault; CIS-hardened baselines
  • Dependency, container and code scanning in every pipeline
  • Approvals and change history recorded automatically
  • An evidence pack you can hand to auditors

05Readiness checklist

  • No secrets live in code or chat
  • Access is reviewed and least-privilege
  • Production changes only go through the pipeline
  • Every change has a recorded approval
  • Vulnerability scans run on every build
  • Environments can be rebuilt from code
  • Logs are centralised and retained
  • You can produce audit evidence in a day
